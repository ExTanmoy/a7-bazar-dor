# 🛒 বাজার দর | BazarDor

**BazarDor** is a Bangla market price tracking web application that helps users explore the prices of everyday essentials in Bangladesh. Browse products by category, track price changes, and compare market prices to make informed shopping decisions.

## 🚀 Live Demo

- **Live Website:** [BazarDor](https://a7-bazar-dor-green.vercel.app/)
- **GitHub Repository:** [ExTanmoy/a7-bazar-dor](https://github.com/ExTanmoy/a7-bazar-dor)

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Next.js** — React framework with App Router
- **TypeScript** — Type-safe development
- **Tailwind CSS** — Responsive styling
- **DaisyUI** — UI components and styling
- **Better Auth** — Authentication for sign up and sign in *(planned)*
- **REST API** — Dynamic product and market price data

## ✨ Key Features

1. **Product Categories:** Explore everyday essentials, including rice, lentils, oil, vegetables, fish, meat, eggs, dairy, and spices.
2. **Price Tracking:** View today's prices and compare them with previous days, weeks, and months.
3. **Price Change Indicators:** Identify products with rising or falling prices and track percentage changes.
4. **Smart Sorting:** Sort products by price from low to high or high to low.
5. **Detailed Price Comparison:** View product details and compare minimum, maximum, and average prices across markets and divisions.

## 📦 Data Source

Product and market price information is fetched from the [BazarDor API](https://openapi.programming-hero.com/api/bazardor).
